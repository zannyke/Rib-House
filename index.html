The Art of Smoke


Where passion meets fire.

At Rib House, we believe that great barbecue is a test of patience. Our signature ribs are dry-rubbed with a proprietary blend of 14 spices, then slow-smoked for up to 12 hours over select hickory and cherry wood logs.

Every steak is hand-selected, USDA Prime, aged for 28 days, and finished on our custom wood-fired grill at 800 degrees to lock in the intense natural flavor and juices.
